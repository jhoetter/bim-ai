## Audit Notes

- `app/bim_ai/energy_lens.py::u_value_for_layers` computes a DIN EN ISO 6946-style U-value readout with `R_si = 0.13` and `R_se = 0.04`, material traceability, and missing-material warnings. It explicitly does not implement GEG, DIN V 18599, BEG/BAFA, thermal bridges, or compliance checks.
- `app/bim_ai/structure_lens.py` is a structural classification, schedule, and export layer only. It performs no Eurocode load, member, snow, wind, concrete, steel, or timber calculations.
- `app/bim_ai/codes.py` contains only three preset door/corridor thresholds and is not a compliance engine.
- `app/bim_ai/code_profile_integrity.py` has advisory fire/accessibility/regional metadata checks, including accessible door width, threshold height, circulation width, sanitary turning zone, missing fire rating metadata, exit door metadata, and firestop metadata. These are profile-controlled sanity checks, not DIN/MBO/LBO compliance implementations.
- `app/bim_ai/area_calculation.py::compute_area_sq_mm` and related helpers implement polygon area, simple inset behavior, and shaft subtraction for model areas. They do not classify DIN 277 areas, WoFlV Wohnfläche, GRZ, GFZ, or Abstandsflächen.
- `app/bim_ai/fire_safety_lens.py` exposes fire compartment, rated element, escape route, firestop penetration, and smoke control schedules from metadata. It does not calculate building classes, required fire resistance, egress compliance, MLAR compliance, or MVStättVO triggers.
- `app/bim_ai/cost_quantity.py` derives model quantities and source-backed cost rows. It accepts `din276Group`/`costGroup` metadata but does not implement DIN 276 cost taxonomy, DIN 276 estimate stages, benchmark ranges, or HOAI fee calculation.
- `app/bim_ai/mep_lens.py` exposes TGA system schedules, room zone metadata, service rows, connector data, opening requests, and electrical-load metadata. It does not implement DIN EN 12831, DIN 1946-6, DVGW W 551, VDE 0100, or DIN EN 806/TRWI calculations.
- `app/bim_ai/sustainability_lca.py` derives layer quantities and embodied-carbon readouts from material impact data. It does not implement full DIN EN 15978 life-cycle module accounting, EU Taxonomy DNSH checks, or DGNB criteria.
